## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Tony Brooks a certified fly casting instructor?**
Yes. Tony Brooks is a Certified Casting Instructor through Fly Fishers International (FFI), a credential reflecting both his technical mastery of fly casting and his dedication to teaching.

**How long has Tony Brooks been guiding?**
Tony has been guiding professionally since 2009 and has been fly fishing for over 50 years.

**What rivers and streams does Tony Brooks guide and specialize in?**
Tony specializes in Western North Carolina mountain streams, including the Davidson River and Pisgah National Forest, and Big Santeetlah Creek, with a focus on dry fly and nymph technique for beginner and intermediate anglers.

**Has Tony Brooks competed in national fly fishing competitions?**
Yes. Tony competed in the 2016 U.S. National Fly Fishing Championship in Lake Placid, NY, and served as a sector judge at the 2024 championship in Cherokee, NC. He is also a member of the competitive fly fishing team, Team Stonefly.

**What organizations is Tony Brooks affiliated with?**
Tony is President and a member of the French Broad Fly Fishers and a member of Team Stonefly, a competitive fly fishing team.
