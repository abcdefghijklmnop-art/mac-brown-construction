Frequently Asked Questions
How much does fly fishing guide school cost?
Tuition for the Fly Fishing Guide School is $4,850 for four days, which includes lunch each day. Lodging and dinner are arranged separately by each student.
Is fly fishing guide school worth the money?
Compared to other elite fly fishing training programs — including week-long, all-inclusive schools costing $12,500 or more — the per-day cost here is meaningfully lower, even after factoring in a student's own lodging and meals.
Do you need to attend a guide school to become a fly fishing guide?
In most states, formal certification isn't legally required to guide. However, a structured program significantly improves your skills, your credibility with outfitters, and your ability to compete for guiding positions against candidates without formal training.
How much do fly fishing guides make?
Entry-level guides working for an outfitter or lodge typically earn $2,400 to $3,000 per month. Experienced independent guides working their home water often earn more, frequently supplementing income with casting instruction and clinics.
What is the class size?
Class size is limited to 8 to 12 students, kept intentionally small so every student receives direct, personal instruction rather than rotating through a large group.
Do I need my own equipment?
No. A wide range of rods, lines, and gear is available to try and compare throughout the program, so students can learn on equipment beyond what they already own.
What happens after graduation?
Graduates leave with a structured assessment of their skills, a clear picture of what to keep working on, and access to job placement resources connecting them with lodges and outfitters looking to hire.
