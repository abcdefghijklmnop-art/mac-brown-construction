⚠️ Issues Found
1. No SPF Record (Both Sites) — HIGH PRIORITY
Neither domain has an SPF (Sender Policy Framework) DNS record. This means anyone can send spoofed emails that appear to come from @macbrownflyfish.com or @flyfishingguideschool.com. Phishing scams targeting your clients are a real risk.
Fix: Add a TXT record to each domain's DNS:
v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
(adjust the include: based on who sends your email — Google, Outlook, etc.)
2. No DMARC Record (Both Sites) — HIGH PRIORITY
DMARC tells email providers what to do when SPF/DKIM fails. Without it, spoofed emails from your domain sail right through.
Fix: Add a TXT record at _dmarc.yourdomain.com:
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:you@yourdomain.com
3. No CAA Record (Both Sites) — MEDIUM
CAA (Certificate Authority Authorization) records restrict which CAs can issue SSL certs for your domain. Without one, any certificate authority in the world could issue a cert for your site (a potential attack vector).
Fix: Add a CAA DNS record allowing only your CA (likely Let's Encrypt or Cloudflare):
0 issue "letsencrypt.org"
0 issue "cloudflare.com"
