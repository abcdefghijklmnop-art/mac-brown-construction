Q1: Where do fly fishing guides meet clients from Highlands NC?
A1: We meet you in Bryson City or Dillsboro — about an 
hour from Highlands. No need to haul gear over the 
mountain. We provide everything you need for the day 
including rods, reels, flies, and waders.

Q2: How far is Highlands NC from the best trout fishing?
A2: About an hour. The Tuckasegee River delayed harvest 
section in Dillsboro, the Nantahala River, and the wild 
streams of Great Smoky Mountains National Park are all 
within an hour of Highlands — and they are among the 
finest trout waters in the eastern United States.

Q3: What rivers do guided fly fishing trips from Highlands NC fish?
A3: The Tuckasegee River delayed harvest section in 
Dillsboro is the most popular — loaded with trout from 
October through May on both float and wade trips. The 
Nantahala River is the top tailwater in the state and 
fishes year-round. Wild streams of Great Smoky Mountains 
National Park — Deep Creek, Noland Creek, Forney Creek, 
and the Oconaluftee — are available for anglers wanting 
a backcountry wild trout experience.

Q4: What is the best time of year for fly fishing near Highlands NC?
A4: April through November is the most popular window, 
but December through March can be excellent on local 
waters. The Tuckasegee delayed harvest section runs 
October through May. The Nantahala and wild Smoky 
Mountain streams fish year-round. Spring and fall are 
the strongest seasons overall.

Q5: Do I need any gear or experience for a guided fly fishing trip from Highlands?
A5: No gear and no experience needed. Mac Brown Fly Fish 
provides everything — rods, reels, flies, leaders, and 
waders. Trips are tailored to your skill level whether 
you are picking up a fly rod for the first time or 
looking to sharpen a specific technique on a specific 
piece of water.
