Q1: Does Mac Brown teach fly casting 
at fly fishing shows?

presenter at The Fly Fishing Show since 
1994 — appearing annually at major show 
cities across the country including 
Raleigh, Somerset, Atlanta, Denver, 
and Pleasanton. Shows provide an 
opportunity to work with students from 
all skill levels in an intensive half-day 
or full-day format and to collaborate 
with other elite instructors and guides 
from around the world.

Q2: What is tension in fly casting 
and why does it matter?
A2: Tension in fly casting refers to 
the degree of tautness maintained in 
the fly line throughout the cast and 
the drift. Understanding constant 
tension, increasing tension, and 
decreasing tension gives the caster 
control over every specialty cast — 
from pile casts that build slack into 
the presentation to tight line Euro 
nymphing that eliminates all slack. 
The same principle applies equally 
on small Smoky Mountain streams and 
30-knot saltwater flats.

Q3: Can I take a fly casting lesson 
at The Fly Fishing Show?
A3: Yes — Mac Brown offers group 
casting clinics and individual casting 
sessions at The Fly Fishing Show in 
Raleigh and other show cities. Check 
the Events Calendar at macbrownflyfish.com 
for upcoming show appearances and 
class schedules. Private casting 
lessons are also available year-round 
in Bryson City, NC and online via 
FaceTime, Zoom, or Skype.

Q4: What fly fishing shows does 
Mac Brown attend?
A4: Mac Brown has been a national 
presenter at The Fly Fishing Show 
since 1994 — appearing in Raleigh NC, 
Somerset NJ, Atlanta GA, Denver CO, 
Pleasanton CA, and other major show 
cities. He teaches the all-day advanced 
fly casting class with Gary Borger at 
multiple show locations each winter 
season. Check macbrownflyfish.com/
events-calendar for current show 
dates and locations.

Q5: Where can I take a fly casting 
lesson with Mac Brown near Raleigh NC?
A5: Mac Brown Fly Fish is based in 
Bryson City, NC — approximately 
4.5 hours from Raleigh. Private 
casting lessons, guided trips, and 
weekend clinics are available 
year-round on the Tuckasegee River, 
Nantahala River, and wild streams 
of Great Smoky Mountains National 
Park. Online casting lessons via 
FaceTime, Zoom, or Skype are 
available at $400 per session for 
Raleigh-area anglers who cannot 
make the drive.
