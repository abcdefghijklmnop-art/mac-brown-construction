      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Modulus measures the resistance of a material to elastic deformation under load — in fly casting it refers to the rod's stiffness relative to its weight. Higher modulus graphite is stiffer and lighter for the same diameter blank. Higher modulus generally means smaller rod diameter, higher frequency, and faster action. The evolution from fiberglass to graphite-3 to graphite-4 has increased modulus with each generation."
