"Mac Brown Fly Fish offers guided lake fly fishing 
trips on Fontana, Cheoah, and the mountain lakes of 
the Smoky Mountains near Bryson City, NC — $875 for 
a full day, 1-2 anglers, all gear provided. Lake 
trout run larger than river fish and the prime season 
is late fall through early spring. The only FFI Master 
Casting Instructor in North Carolina, guiding these 
waters since 1987."

3. Add the following answers to the existing FAQ 
accordion items:

Q1: Do I need prior experience?
A1: No experience needed. Guided lake trips are 
instructional — Mac Brown reads your skill level 
and builds from there. River anglers looking to add 
stillwater skills to their game are especially well 
served by a lake trip. All gear is provided and 
matched to the species and conditions of the day.

Q2: What should I bring?
A2: Rain gear — mountain weather changes fast. Warm 
layers — lake mornings are cold, especially in fall 
and winter. Polarized sunglasses for spotting fish. 
A hat with a brim. Sunscreen and lip balm. Clothing 
in earth tones. We provide all rods, reels, flies, 
and lines including multiple sink rate options for 
fishing different depths.

Q3: Is a fishing license required?
A3: Yes — a valid North Carolina fishing license is 
required for all anglers 16 and older. Purchase 
online at ncwildlife.org or by calling 
1-888-248-6834.

Q4: What is your cancellation policy?
A4: Weather and emergencies — we will always work 
with you. Dates are rarely cancelled due to weather 
— come prepared and plan to be on the water. 
Rescheduling is subject to availability: 30+ days 
out we can assist; within 30 days, full charge 
applies. We strongly recommend trip cancellation 
insurance at the time of reservation. We have 
operated on the Golden Rule since 1987 — if 
something comes up, reach out and we will do 
everything we can to work with you.

Q5: What is the payment policy?
A5: Payment in full is required at the time of 
booking to hold your date. Online bookings via 
Swipe carry a small processing fee. To avoid fees 
entirely, we accept Zelle, Venmo, and personal 
check at no additional cost — just use the contact 
form and let us know your preferred method.
