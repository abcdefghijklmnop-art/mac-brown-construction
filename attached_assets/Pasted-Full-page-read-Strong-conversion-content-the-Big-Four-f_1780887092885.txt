Q2: How do I choose a quality fly 
fishing guide in North Carolina?
A2: Look for independent guides with 
professional certifications — FFI casting 
instructor credentials, ACA rowing 
certifications, and verifiable experience.

Q3: Is a wind knot really caused 
by the wind?
A3: No — a wind knot is always a fly 
casting fault. The term is an age-old 
excuse that the sport has perpetuated 
to be more palatable. Wind knots are 
tailing loops — caused by abrupt or 
early force application creating a 
concave rod tip path. The wind may 
make a tailing loop worse but it does 
not cause it. Understanding this 
distinction is the first step toward 
eliminating the fault. Experienced 
certified casting instructors can 
diagnose and correct tailing loops 
in a single session.

Q4: What makes an independent fly 
fishing guide better than a fly 
shop guide?
A4: Independent guides who have guided 
the same water for decades develop an 
depth of knowledge — about fish behavior, 
water reading, seasonal patterns, and 
teaching technique — that cannot be 
replicated by seasonal fly shop employees. 
Their livelihood depends entirely on the 
quality of every single trip. No one 
works harder for your experience than 
someone whose reputation is built one 
client at a time over decades. Mac Brown 
has been guiding independently in Bryson 
City, NC since 1987 — the original fly 
fishing guide service in Western NC.

Q5: What are the four key skills 
taught on every Mac Brown guided trip?
A5: The Big Four taught on every Mac 
Brown Fly Fish guided trip are: fly 
casting and direction change — covering 
water efficiently from any position; 
line control — dead drift presentations 
and active fly manipulation; hook setting 
— the horizontal strip set that produces 
the highest hookup percentage for trout; 
and pressuring fish — applying side 
pressure to tire fish quickly and land 
them without dropping them at the net. 
These four skills are taught regardless 
of weather, water conditions, or fish 
activity — every trip, every client, 
every year since 1987.
