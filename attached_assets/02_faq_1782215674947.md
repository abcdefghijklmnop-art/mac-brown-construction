## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Effective Fly Casting Technique (EFCT)?**
EFCT is a casting system developed by Michael Mauri that's built around natural body movement and efficient mechanics, designed to simplify the learning curve for fly casters of any skill level.

**What casting disciplines does Michael Mauri teach?**
Michael teaches single-hand casting, double-hand (Spey) casting, and overhead casting, working with anglers from complete beginners to advanced casters.

**Where can I take a casting lesson with Michael Mauri?**
Michael offers private casting lessons and guided trips out of Stuart, Florida through Mauri Flyfishing, and also teaches at fly-fishing shows and destination casting schools across Europe, Canada, and the United States.

**Does Michael Mauri design fly rods?**
Yes. Michael consults as a design engineer for a leading fly rod manufacturer and also designs and handcrafts his own line of fly rods.

**How long has Michael Mauri been teaching fly casting?**
Michael has been teaching fly casting internationally since 1998, with decades of experience guiding and instructing across multiple continents.
