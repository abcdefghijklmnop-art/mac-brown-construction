About Casting Angles
Casting Angles is the most comprehensive study of fly casting mechanics ever assembled in a single volume — written by North Carolina's only FFI Master Casting Instructor after nearly four decades of teaching, guiding, and competing at the highest levels of the sport. Drawing on tournament casting principles refined since the late 1850s, the book breaks down the physics, mechanics, and presentation strategies behind every cast in a way that is direct, original, and immediately useful on the water.
From loop control to aerial mending, from the double haul to advanced presentation angles — Casting Angles covers the full spectrum with a depth and honesty that no other casting book matches. Readers from Australia to New Zealand to Europe have called it the most thorough casting resource they have encountered anywhere.
"Whether you are a beginner building your first foundation or an experienced caster looking to finally understand why your cast does what it does — this book will change the way you think about fly casting permanently."
