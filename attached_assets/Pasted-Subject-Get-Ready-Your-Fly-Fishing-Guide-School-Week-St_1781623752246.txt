Subject: Get Ready — Your Fly Fishing Guide School Week Starts Soon!

Hello Guide School Class,

This is your final prep email before we kick off what is going to be an outstanding week of learning, fishing, and all things fly fishing. A number of you have reached out asking what to bring — great question. Check out the full gear list at flyfishingguideschool.com/necessary-items-fly-fishing-guide-school, and think of it like packing for a full fishing holiday: flies, tippet, vest, wading boots, rods, reels — the works. If you tie, feel free to bring your vise. We will work fly tying into the week to lay down a solid foundation of the core concepts.

Mid-October weather in the Smokies can swing wildly, so come prepared for everything — rain gear, warm layers, and a sense of adventure. One item that is absolutely essential: a notebook. Each morning opens in a classroom setting packed with information you will want to capture and carry with you the rest of your career.

Note: Daily locations for Days 4 and 5 will be confirmed the evening before based on water and weather conditions. Flexibility is part of the guide life — and part of great teaching. Stay tuned to your email each evening.

I am genuinely excited to meet this group and spend the week deep in fly fishing — the casting, the guiding, the reading of water, the business, and all the craft that makes this sport so endlessly rewarding. Safe travels to everyone, and I will see you on the water.
