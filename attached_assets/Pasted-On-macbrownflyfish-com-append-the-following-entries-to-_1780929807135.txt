      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Drift in fly casting is a supplementary motion that takes place during the pause of the stroke for repositioning the rod. Drift offers an advantageous position for shooting line at the completion of the casting stroke. An upward drift toward the unrolling line opens up the casting stroke and arc angle, making it easier to shoot line. Drift is different from creep — drift is intentional repositioning, creep is an unintentional fault."
