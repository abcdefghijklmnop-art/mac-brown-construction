FAQ — add before footer as expandable accordion:

Q1: What are the four stages of 
successful training in fly fishing?
A1: Stage 1 — Unconscious Incompetence: 
you don't know what you don't know. 
You think you're ready to fish after 
watching YouTube. Stage 2 — Conscious 
Incompetence: the light comes on and 
you realize how much there is to learn. 
This is the most important stage — 
humility opens the door to real 
improvement. Stage 3 — Conscious 
Competence: you know what to do but 
must think about it deliberately. 
Stage 4 — Unconscious Competence: 
the cast, the mend, the read — all 
happen instinctively in flow state.

Q2: How long does it take to reach 
unconscious competence in fly fishing?
A2: It varies widely — but the single 
biggest accelerator is quality 
instruction early. A fly fisher who 
receives certified casting instruction 
at the beginning builds correct 
fundamentals from day one and reaches 
conscious competence far faster than 
one who self-teaches and spends years 
reinforcing faults. Mac Brown's guided 
trips and instruction programs are 
designed to move anglers through 
stages two and three as efficiently 
as possible.
