FAQ — add before footer as expandable accordion:

Q1: Where is the best fly fishing 
near Highlands NC?
A1: The best trout fishing waters 
near Highlands, NC are approximately 
one hour away in Bryson City and the 
surrounding Swain County watershed. 
The Tuckasegee River delayed harvest 
section, the Nantahala River tailwater, 
and the wild streams of Great Smoky 
Mountains National Park and Nantahala 
National Forest all offer world-class 
trout fishing. Swain County — over 85% 
government land — is the trout mecca 
of the southeastern United States.

Q2: Is there fly fishing directly 
in Highlands NC?
A2: Yes — but with limitations. The 
Cullasaja River flows from Lake Sequoyah 
below Highlands with some trout water, 
but the lake warms significantly during 
summer which negatively impacts 
downstream trout fishing. The Chattooga 
River at the Iron Bridge outside of 
Highlands offers better trout water — 
challenging technical fishing with 
heavy canopy, diverse currents, and 
large undercut rocks. Best suited for 
experienced fly fishers. The finest 
trout waters are an hour's drive to 
Bryson City.

Q3: How far is Bryson City from 
Highlands NC?
A3: Bryson City is approximately one 
hour from Highlands, NC via US-64 
West through Cashiers and Franklin. 
Mac Brown Fly Fish has been guiding 
Highlands-area visitors to the 
Tuckasegee River, Nantahala River, 
and wild Smoky Mountain streams since 
1987. Most Highlands visitors make 
the drive for a full-day guided float 
or wade trip — the quality of the 
fishery more than justifies the drive.

Q4: What is the best season for 
fly fishing near Highlands NC?
A4: April through November is the 
most popular season for guided trips 
near Highlands — with spring (March 
through May) producing the strongest 
hatch activity and fish counts on 
the Tuckasegee and Nantahala. 
December through March is excellent 
on the delayed harvest sections and 
tailwaters with midge and BWO 
activity on mild overcast days. 
The Tuckasegee delayed harvest 
section runs October through May 
with the best float trip conditions 
of the entire year.

Q5: Does Mac Brown Fly Fish guide 
trips for Highlands NC visitors?
A5: Yes — Mac Brown Fly Fish has 
been guiding Highlands, Cashiers, 
and surrounding area visitors to 
the finest trout waters in Western 
North Carolina since 1987. Guided 
float trips and wade trips are 
available year-round on the 
Tuckasegee River, Nantahala River, 
and wild streams of the Great Smoky 
Mountains. All gear provided. Call 
(828) 736-1469 or use the reservation 
form at macbrownflyfish.com to book.
