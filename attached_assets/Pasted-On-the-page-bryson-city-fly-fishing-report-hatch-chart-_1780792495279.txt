On the page /bryson-city-fly-fishing-report-hatch-chart 
on macbrownflyfish.com build an automated fishing 
conditions widget that does the following:

1. Fetches real-time data from the USGS Water 
Services API for the following gauge stations:
- Tuckasegee River at Bryson City: site 03460000
- Nantahala River: site 03503000

Pull CFS flow and water temperature for each.

2. Fetches current air temperature and forecast 
for Bryson City NC from the National Weather 
Service API (no key required):
https://api.weather.gov/points/35.4265,-83.4473

3. Displays the following conditions for each river:

TUCKASEGEE RIVER:
CFS Flow Conditions:
- Below 500 CFS: Too low to float — fish the 
  freestone streams in the Smokies instead
- 500–2,000 CFS: Float trip recommended — 
  ideal conditions
- 2,000–4,000 CFS: Float trips still fine — 
  experienced conditions
- Above 4,000 CFS: No floating — bank fishing 
  only from safe access points

NANTAHALA RIVER:
CFS Flow Conditions:
- Generation off (low CFS): Excellent wading — 
  stay river-left on the road side, be aware 
  of release schedules
- Generation on (higher CFS): Float trips 
  recommended — check TVA release schedule 
  before wading

BOTH RIVERS — Water Temperature Rating:
- Below 45°F: Midges and BWOs — nymphing and 
  wet flies most productive
- 45–55°F: Early hatches beginning — Quill 
  Gordons, Blue Quills, Black Stones — dry 
  fly fishing becomes viable
- 55–65°F: PRIME CONDITIONS — all techniques 
  firing, best dry fly fishing of the year
- 65°F and above: Stop fishing trout — switch 
  to warm water species (smallmouth bass). 
  Fish early morning only before temps rise.
- Above 72°F: Rest the water entirely

4. Display a fishing condition summary card 
for each river showing:
- Current CFS
- Current water temperature °F
- Current air temperature °F
- Float or Wade recommendation
- Technique recommendation
- Condition rating (Prime / Good / Fair / 
  Poor / Rest the Water)
- Safety note when applicable (high water 
  bank fishing only, Nantahala release 
  schedule warning)

5. Add a "Last Updated" timestamp.

6. Add a safety disclaimer below the widget:
"Always check TVA and Duke Energy generation 
schedules before wading the Nantahala River. 
When in doubt, stay on the road side of the 
river for safe exit. Above 4,000 CFS on the 
Tuckasegee — float trips are off. Bank fishing 
only from safe access points."

7. Data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes.

8. Style the widget to match the existing page 
design — clean cards, color coded by condition 
rating (green = Prime, yellow = Good, orange = 
Fair, red = Poor/Rest), mobile friendly.

Then run bash push.sh to deploy. No other changes.
