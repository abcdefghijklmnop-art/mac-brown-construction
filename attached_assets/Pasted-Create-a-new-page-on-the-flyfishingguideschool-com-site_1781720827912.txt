This school is for anyone who wants to spend a few days learning to fish well — first-timers who've never held a fly rod, weekend anglers ready to feel more confident, retirees looking for something new, and lifelong fishers who just want to finally understand what they're doing out there. You don't need to want a career on the water. You just need to want to learn. Classes are small, the teaching is patient, and the water is some of the best in the Southeast.

H3: Casting That Feels Natural
You'll learn to cast smoothly and accurately, without getting buried in technical terms. We teach with patience, not jargon — by the end, it feels like something your body just knows how to do.

H3: Understanding the River
We'll teach you to look at moving water and read it — where fish are likely holding, what they're doing, and how to approach quietly and thoughtfully.

H3: Knowing What the Fish Are Eating
No need to memorize anything in Latin. We'll show you simply how to notice what's around you and pick a fly that makes sense for the moment.

H3: A Few Knots, Practiced Until They're Second Nature
Just the handful you actually need, taught simply enough to stick.
