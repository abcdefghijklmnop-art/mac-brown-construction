Spey Elite Masterclass Clinic
Two-Handed Fly Casting School — Bryson City, NC
Great Smoky Mountains · Tuckasegee River · All Skill Levels Welcome

The most powerful, elegant, and addictive cast in fly fishing is closer than you think.
Most anglers who watch Spey casting for the first time feel one of two things: pure inspiration or quiet intimidation. The sweeping D-loop, the effortless distance, the way a well-executed cast unrolls across a wide river like it was pulled from the water itself — it looks like something reserved for a very specific kind of angler. It isn't. With the right instruction, two-handed casting is not only learnable but transformational — opening up water, techniques, and a dimension of fly fishing that most anglers never experience.
The Spey Elite Masterclass Clinic in Bryson City, North Carolina is the Southeast's premier destination for two-handed fly casting instruction. Whether you are picking up a switch rod for the first time, looking to refine mechanics you've been working on for years, or pursuing FFI Two-Handed Casting Instructor certification, this clinic delivers the instruction, the environment, and the expertise to take your casting to a level you didn't know was available to you.

Who This Clinic Is For

Single-hand casters ready to add a powerful new dimension to their fly fishing
Trout anglers wanting to reach water and make presentations currently beyond their range
Steelhead and salmon anglers building the foundational skills the pursuit demands
Guides and shop staff pursuing FFI Two-Handed Casting Instructor certification
Anyone who has attempted Spey casting on their own and hit a frustrating wall

If you have ever watched someone Spey cast and thought — I want to do that — this weekend is your answer.

What You Will Learn
Anchor Placement & D-Loop Formation
The foundation every Spey cast is built on. Most self-taught two-handed casters struggle here without knowing it — and every downstream problem traces back to this one upstream cause. Get anchor placement right and the rest of the cast begins to make sense immediately.
Skagit Casting
Short, powerful, and built for heavy flies, sink tips, and tight bankside quarters. The Skagit system is the workhorse of Spey fishing — practical, adaptable, and devastatingly effective when executed correctly.
Scandi Casting
Long, elegant, and built for distance and delicacy on open water. Understanding when to reach for Skagit versus Scandi — and why — is as important as mastering both systems.
Switch Rod Techniques
The versatile bridge between single-hand and full Spey. An increasingly essential tool for trout anglers on big water and one of the fastest-growing disciplines in the sport.
Sustained Anchor Casts
Snake roll, Perry Poke, Snap-T, Double Spey — each cast has its conditions, its logic, and its specific advantages. You will learn not just how to execute each one but precisely when and why to reach for it.
Running Line & Shooting Head Systems
How different head configurations change casting behavior, and how to adjust your technique to match the system you are fishing — a critical skill most anglers never fully develop.
Aerial Mending & Presentation
Getting the fly to the fish is only half the job. Two-handed rod mending opens up drift presentations and current management that single-hand anglers simply cannot achieve. This alone is worth the weekend.
FFI Two-Handed Certification Preparation
For candidates pursuing the credential — complete task preparation, mock testing, and diagnostic feedback from FFI Level 2 examiners with a near-100% certification pass rate over many years.

The Venue
The Tuckasegee River running through Bryson City, North Carolina is one of the finest Spey teaching venues in the eastern United States. Wide enough for full Spey casting, varied enough to practice every anchor position and presentation angle, and beautiful enough to make every hour on the water feel like a privilege. The rivers of Western North Carolina — the Tuckasegee, the Nantahala, and the tailwaters of the broader Smoky Mountains region — provide real fishing conditions that translate directly to success anywhere you travel to cast a two-handed rod.
Bryson City sits within easy reach of Asheville, Cherokee, and the heart of the Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Many participants extend their stay to fish the surrounding water — some of the finest wild trout streams in the eastern United States.

The Instruction
Mac Brown is North Carolina's only FFI Master Casting Instructor and an FFI Level 2 examiner with decades of two-handed casting experience across multiple disciplines and continents. His team brings the same depth of knowledge and passion for teaching that has produced a near-100% certification pass rate for CI and MCI candidates over many years. World-class guest instructors with international Spey credentials join select clinics throughout the year — bringing perspectives shaped by the rivers of Scotland, Scandinavia, and the Pacific Northwest directly to the mountains of Western North Carolina.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need my own Spey rod?
Equipment recommendations will be provided upon registration. Loaners may be available — contact us to discuss your situation.
Is this for beginners or advanced casters?
Both. The clinic is structured to meet every participant where they are and move them forward significantly from that starting point.
What is the difference between Skagit and Scandi casting?
Skagit is a short, powerful system ideal for heavy flies and tight conditions. Scandi is a longer, more elegant system built for distance and delicacy. Both are covered in full.
Will this prepare me for FFI Two-Handed certification?
Absolutely. Full certification preparation is a core component of the clinic for candidates who want it.
What rivers will we fish and practice on?
Primarily the Tuckasegee River in Bryson City, with additional venues depending on conditions and group focus.
