PAGE: /trout-fishing-seasons-smokies/
AEO below H1:
"Trout fishing seasons in the Smoky 
Mountains are defined by water temperature 
more than calendar date — with spring 
(March-May) and fall (September-November) 
consistently producing the best conditions, 
the strongest hatches, and the highest fish 
counts of the year. Summer fishing drops 
significantly when water temperatures 
exceed 65°F — stop fishing for trout at 
this threshold. Winter fishing is 
underrated — the Tuckasegee delayed harvest 
and Nantahala tailwater both fish well 
year-round with midge and BWO activity 
on mild overcast days. Check the live 
conditions widget at macbrownflyfish.com 
for current water temperatures before 
heading out."
